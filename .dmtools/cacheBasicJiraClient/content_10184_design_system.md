## Brand & Style

The design system powers a dual-experience hospitality ecosystem bridging sensory guest-facing table ordering with high-density back-of-house venue management. It rejects sterile monochrome enterprise tropes in favor of an appetizing, warm-culinary aesthetic influenced by modern Scandinavian craft, bistro warmth, and technical SaaS rigor.

The target audience encompasses two distinct archetypes:
1. **The Dining Guest**: Patrons ordering in varied lighting conditions (sunlit patios, low-lit cocktail lounges, crowded bistros) requiring rapid scannability, legible typography, high-contrast dietary safety signals, and intuitive touch interaction.
2. **Hospitality Operators & Staff**: Floor managers, chefs, and line expedite operators who demand maximum information density, zero-latency feedback, clear order lifecycle color states, and keyboard/touch-friendly task completion.

The brand style merges **warm minimalism** (sunlit stone canvases, roasted terracotta interactive nodes, crisp card staging) with **functional modernism** (tabular numeric tracking, monospace order identifiers, and dark slate administrative anchors). Visual hierarchy eliminates cognitive friction, guiding guests effortlessly toward checkout while empowering staff with uncompromising operational control.

## Colors

The color palette centers on culinary resonance, psychological warmth, and strict semantic clarity across high-pressure environments.

- **Primary (`#e0533c`)**: Terracotta Coral acts as the primary interactive catalyst for ordering actions, price emphasis, checkout buttons, and active category switches. It evokes roasted embers, rich reduction sauces, and warm clay earthenware.
- **Secondary (`#d97706`)**: Amber Tangerine provides warmth for pending alerts, incoming orders, tips, and chef highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#0f766e`)**: Deep Teal adds a grounded organic counterweight for operational success states, inventory filters, and reporting dashboards.
- **Neutral (`#1c1917`)**: Deep Charcoal Espresso replaces harsh pure black, providing crisp contrast without visual fatigue on both warm stone backgrounds and crisp card surfaces.

### Semantic Status Architecture
Kitchen display systems and ordering states depend on immediate peripheral readability:
- **Incoming / Unacknowledged**: Amber (`#d97706`) on Cream Yellow (`#fef3c7`).
- **Kitchen Cooking**: Cobalt Indigo (`#2563eb`) on Pale Indigo (`#dbeafe`).
- **Ready for Service**: Forest Emerald (`#16a34a`) on Soft Mint (`#dcfce7`).
- **86'd / Sold Out / Error**: Crimson (`#dc2626`) on Soft Rose (`#fee2e2`).

### Administrative Shell
The SaaS control room utilizes dark slate tones (`#0f172a` and `#1e293b`) for side navigation and live ticket headers to anchor the eye and reduce glare during night shifts.

## Typography

The typographic scale combines the geometric warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the utilitarian clarity of **Inter** and the tabular precision of **JetBrains Mono**.

### Roles & Execution
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: Drives display headlines, dish names, pricing points, category pills, and table markers. Tight negative letter spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.025em`) delivers an editorial, restaurant-grade presence that holds together in loud, vibrant environments.
- **Inter**: Governs long-form dish descriptions, complex allergen disclosures, ingredient lists, and data table layouts. Its neutral optical qualities ensure peak legibility on small phone screens.
- **JetBrains Mono**: Dedicated to order ticket numbers (`#B-409`), table tags (`TBL-04`), POS codes, and live preparation timers (`14:32`).
- **Tabular Numerics Rule**: Any UI element displaying monetary sums, countdown timers, or item multipliers must enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` to prevent layout shaking during increment/decrement interactions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy adapts to two distinct screen paradigms:
1. **Guest Touchpoint (Constrained Fluid Mobile)**: Locked to a maximum reading container of 480px centered on viewports. It employs a single-column layout with 16px lateral padding and 12px card-to-card rhythm. Generous vertical breathing room highlights food photography without visual noise.
2. **Venue SaaS Cockpit (Multi-Column Fluid Grid)**: Uses an adaptable 12-column workspace or responsive Kanban board. Columns transition from single-column on mobile/tablet to 3–4 persistent operational swimlanes (New, Prep, Ready, Expedited) on desktop and dedicated touch monitors (1024px+).

### Spacing Scale
- Micro-spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) separates dietary badges, price tags, and inline modifier chips.
- Component-level spacing (`space-md`, `space-lg`) regulates card padding, input fields, and category pill bars.
- Layout-level spacing (`space-xl`, `gutter`, `margin`) establishes visual rhythm between menu categories, sheet headers, and administrative panels.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered tonal planes complemented by soft ambient shadows rather than harsh borders or aggressive drop-shadows.

### Surface Stacking Tiers
1. **Base Canvas**: Culinary stone canvas (`#faf7f5`) serves as the foundation for the guest experience.
2. **Raised Surfaces**: Menu item cards, customization sheets, and category containers use crisp white (`#ffffff`).
3. **Floating & Sticky Elements**: Active bottom cart summaries and top navigation bars rest above the viewport using subtle tinted shadows (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.08)`).
4. **Modal Sheets**: Bottom modifier sheets apply a backdrop blur (12px) paired with a muted dark overlay (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)`) to focus guest attention on dish personalization.

### Administrative Contrast
In SaaS management interfaces, depth transitions into a tonal structure: deep slate container bases (`#0f172a`), raised working cards (`#1e293b`), and low-contrast borders (`#334155`) for ticket boundaries.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded** (Level 2) shape language, balancing casual hospitality warmth with modern interface efficiency.

- **Base Corner Radius (0.5rem / 8px)**: Applied to table cells, small dietary tags, counter controls, and admin inputs.
- **Large Corner Radius (1rem / 16px)**: Applied to dish list cards, metric cards, order tickets, and promotional banners.
- **Extra Large Corner Radius (1.5rem / 24px)**: Reserved for mobile bottom sheets (top corners) and floating order summary docks.
- **Pill Shape (`rounded-full` / 9999px)**: Dedicated to category navigation chips, table identifiers (`Table 04`), dietary capsules (`VEGAN`, `GF`), and primary floating action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Order Button**: Solid terracotta coral (`#e0533c`) with crisp white typography. Height is fixed at 52px on mobile to guarantee thumb reach. Subtly tinted shadow (`0 4px 14px rgba(224, 83, 60, 0.28)`) with a quick 150ms active depression (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Outlined Button**: 1.5px solid border in `#d6d3d1`, crisp white background, `#1c1917` text.
- **Quantity Stepper Controls**: Circular or pill containers with soft stone fills (`#ede3dc`) and terracotta bold symbols (`+` / `−`). Minimum touch footprint: 44x44px.

### Category Pills & Dietary Badges
- **Category Filter Pills**: Horizontally scrollable bar with hidden scrollbars. Inactive state: `#ffffff` background with 1px hairline border `#e7e5e4`. Active state: solid `#e0533c` background with bold white text.
- **Dietary & Allergen Badges**: Micro capsules (`label-caps`) with 4px 10px padding:
  - *Vegan / Plant*: `#dcfce7` fill with `#15803d` text.
  - *Spicy*: `#ffe4e6` fill with `#be123c` text.
  - *Gluten-Free*: `#fef9c3` fill with `#a16207` text.
  - *House Signature*: `#ffedd5` fill with `#c2410c` text.

### Dish Cards
- **Mobile Menu Card**: Horizontal flex card, 120px height, crisp white surface, rounded-xl (16px). Left side contains title (`Headline-SM`), two-line description (`Body-SM`), and tabular price tag (`Price-LG`). Right side holds a 96x96px rounded thumbnail with an overlaid circular terracotta add button.
- **86'd / Sold-Out Card**: Opacity drops to 0.55, image applies grayscale filter, and an angled slate badge states "Sold Out".

### Modifier & Customization Bottom Sheet
- Surfaces from the bottom with 24px top rounded corners.
- Groups options into mandatory selections ("Required - Choose 1") indicated with bold terracotta and optional toppings ("Optional - Pick up to 3") indicated in muted slate.
- Radio and checkbox selectors use tactile pill styles that highlight with peachy-terracotta background washes (`#ffede8`) and price delta tags (`+$1.50`).

### Live Order Tickets (KDS Kanban)
- Fixed-width cards (280px–320px) with color-coded top accent status bars (Amber for New, Blue for Cooking, Green for Ready).
- Header highlights table number (`Table 04`) in `code-mono` alongside elapsed tabular timers.
- Items feature quick-tap strikethrough logic for expediting lines, with high-priority modifiers (e.g., `ALLERGY: PEANUT`) highlighted in high-contrast callout boxes.
- Bump action button spans the ticket footer for single-tap lifecycle progression.
